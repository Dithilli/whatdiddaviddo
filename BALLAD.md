# One Hundred Percent

A power ballad in the key of self-inflicted incident review.

**Style prompt for the music tool:**
`80s power ballad, male lead vocal, clean arpeggiated electric guitar intro, big reverb snare entering verse 2, soaring twin-guitar solo, key change before final chorus, anthemic gang vocals, 72 BPM building to 84, Journey / Foreigner / Bon Jovi`

---

## [Verse 1]

I built it on a Tuesday, twenty-five hundred lines
Invoices and contacts, every field aligned
It came back green, it came back clean
Prettiest little nothing that I've ever seen

## [Pre-Chorus]

'Cause the bar don't lie — the bar just moves
The bar don't know what it's got to prove

## [Chorus]

**It said one hundred percent!**
**It found nothing at all!**
Nobody got paged 'cause nothing ever fell
A green light burning in an empty hall
I would've taken failure — failure I can tell
**It said one hundred percent...**
**and it found nothing at all**

## [Verse 2]

A hundred thirty-four doors and we could open four
Finance, Insurance, everything they're paying for
Not locked, not lost, not hidden from the light —
they were *invisible*, and that's not the same thing, all right

## [Pre-Chorus]

And the ghosts came back with no names on
Every record empty, every ID gone

## [Chorus]

**It said one hundred percent!**
**It found nothing at all!**
A whole continent connected to a wall
Never synced — not once, not ever, not at all
I would've taken failure — failure I can tell
**It said one hundred percent...**
**and it found nothing at all**

## [Bridge — half time, just piano and voice]

I sent a five-oh-two
when the fault was mine to own
I blamed the whole world's weather
for a storm I made alone

*(building)*

So I labelled two of them: **do not merge**
'Cause knowing what don't work is still a word
And Thursday had three meetings all at once —
and twenty-five commits, and I don't know how

## [Final Chorus]

Now it says **four hundred** — and it means it when it speaks
It fails closed, it fails loud, it doesn't fail for weeks
Twenty-six went out the door, sixteen still in flight
Eight new tickets on the board and every one of them is right

**It'll never say one hundred**
**when it's found nothing at all**
**Not on my watch**
**Not anymore**
**Not at all**
